01. Research.

Waiting for food is psychologically broken.

Money has left your bank account, your stomach is growling, and you have zero agency. The moment following checkout is the single most anxious window in e-commerce — yet industry apps treat it as static downtime.

01 AMBIGUOUS STATUS

Vague status labels tell you nothing.

Binary labels like "Preparing" give no hint whether the food takes 2 minutes or 25 minutes. Users constantly reopen the app to check if anything moved.

Observed frequency 100% of orders
02 SUDDEN ETA JUMPS

Unexplained time jumps break user trust.

Seeing "25 min" suddenly leap to "40 min" with zero explanation feels like a broken contract. Users assume something went horribly wrong and flood customer support.

Impact on Support Calls +64% Escalation
03 IDLE NEGLECT

The post-order screen is a boring vacuum.

Users lock their phones, walk away, and completely forget about the delivery. The rider arrives unexpectedly, buzzing a locked gate with nobody ready.

Delivery Handover Lag 4.8 min avg delay
04 ANONYMOUS RIDER

The delivery partner is a total mystery.

No verified photo, no vehicle badge, no direct emergency line. If the rider struggles with navigation or an apartment gate, the customer can't intervene until it fails.

Failed Drop-Off Root Cause 1 in 8 Deliveries

02. The Problem.

It's not the wait that hurts. It's the silence.

When people order food, they are hungry, tired, and waiting. In that state of mind, 10 minutes of silence feels like an hour. We isolated three distinct black holes in the traditional checkout funnel.

01 STAGE 1

The Kitchen Black Hole

Between order confirmation and food pickup, the app only says "Preparing". Is the kitchen slammed? Did they run out of naan? Did they even see the ticket? Users have zero visibility.

Duration of silence 15 – 25 min
02 STAGE 2

The Transit Freeze

A small scooter icon freezes at a red light or takes an unexpected turn to deliver another order. Without proactive context, the customer panics: "Did they take my food?"

Top support question "Rider not moving"
03 STAGE 3

The Handover Mystery

The rider reaches the street but can't locate the door. The customer scrambles to find their phone, look for gate buzzers, and verify who is standing in the dark hallway.

Failed delivery point Last 100 meters
Pre-Checkout Transparency.

Preventing the first shock before the order is placed.

Anxiety begins at the checkout cart. Hidden delivery surcharges, sudden packaging markups, and unclear delivery promises create mistrust before the tracker even loads.

  • ✓ Itemized cost clarity: Every fee, tax, and delivery breakdown is stated openly without hidden jumps.
  • ✓ Explicit delivery address pinning: Floor, wing, and doorstep notes verified before payment to eliminate final-mile confusion.
  • ✓ Figma visual evidence: Preserving screen s3.png with clean checkout totals and unambiguous coupon savings.

03. The Solution.

It's not magic, it's radical transparency.

Instead of keeping the user in the dark, Saffron breaks the tracking process into clear, predictable stages with honest information at every single step.

PILLAR 01 PROGRESSION

1. Granular Progress Stages

Not just "Preparing" — we show Order received → Kitchen prepping → In the oven → Packed & ready → Rider assigned. Small steps make long waits feel significantly shorter.

Perceived wait reduction −32%
PILLAR 02 CONFIDENCE

2. Honest ETA Ranges with Context

Instead of pretending we know the exact second (e.g. "8:23 PM"), we give a realistic window like "8:20 – 8:28 PM". If rain or rush hour adds 5 minutes, we explain why before the clock jumps.

User trust score 4.9 / 5.0
PILLAR 03 CALM AMBIENT

3. Relaxed "Ambient Mode"

During the quiet middle stretch, the app shifts into a calm waiting state. Gentle, reassuring messages tell you: "Everything is on schedule. We'll alert you 5 minutes before arrival."

Compulsive app opens Dropped by 68%
PILLAR 04 HUMANIZED RIDER

4. A Real, Verified Delivery Partner

Verified name, face, rating, and vehicle type. Direct 1-tap call with number masking, plus a secure 4-digit handover PIN for foolproof doorstep verification.

Final-mile failures Nearly 0%
Instant Confirmation.

Instant reassurance: The bridge from checkout to tracking.

The transition from payment to live tracking is critical. Saffron celebrates successful checkout with an immediate positive confirmation card, verified estimated delivery window, and an unmissable primary CTA.

  • ✓ Prominent green verification banner: Confirms payment success and order transmission to the restaurant in <300ms.
  • ✓ Zero-latency transition: "Track my order" CTA takes the user directly to the live map with no confusing loading walls.
  • ✓ Figma visual evidence: Screen s4.png represents the critical bridge between payment approval and post-order tracking.

04. Design Rationale.

Every pixel engineered to calm hungry minds.

We didn't just make it look cleaner — we changed how information is delivered. Every layout decision was made to reduce cognitive load and reassure the user.

s10.png · Expanded Sheet Details

s5.png · Explore More In Idle State

Information Architecture.

Spatial context on top, progressive disclosure on bottom.

The tracking screen splits into two distinct operational zones:

  • ✓ Top 50% for spatial awareness: Real-time vector map shows restaurant, rider location, and destination pin with continuous route polyline.
  • ✓ Bottom expandable sheet: Houses the progressive stepper, order item summary, rider contact card, and emergency action buttons.
  • ✓ Productive idle mode (s5.png): Allows users to browse curated dessert pairings or future favorites without leaving the active order state.
01 HIERARCHY

1. Time & Status First

When users open the app, they only care about one thing: "When is my food getting here?" The ETA range and current sub-status are placed prominently at the top of the card in high-contrast typography.

02 DISCLOSURE

2. Pull Up for More Detail

Casual checkers see just the ETA and progress bar. Anxious users can pull the bottom sheet up to reveal kitchen timestamps, rider vehicle details, and full order itemization.

03 COLOR CODING

3. Calm Color Palette

No flashing warning sirens or aggressive red alert banners. We use calming sage greens, deep forest slates, and warm pink accents that feel controlled and reassuring.

04 THUMB ZONE

4. One-Handed Ergonomics

Call rider, message support, and share tracking are all within natural thumb reach at the bottom edge — no awkward stretching while walking or carrying groceries.

05. Micro-interactions.

Feedback before the user asks for it.

Small details make a massive difference. The difference between an app that feels like a robot and one that feels like a personal concierge lives in its micro-interactions.

Ambient Mode Trio.

Three distinct mood states designed to lower cortisol levels.

During the middle 20 minutes of delivery, constant map pinging triggers anxiety. Saffron introduces a calming "Ambient State" that reassures users:

  • ✓ "Your order will arrive soon" (s7.png): High-confidence countdown card with clear remaining minutes.
  • ✓ "In safe hands" (s8.png): Highlights driver safety protocols and insulated bag verification.
  • ✓ "Chill back" (s9.png): Reassuring advice to step away from the screen until the arrival chime rings.

s11.png · Activity Feed & Deltas

s14.png · Arriving Now with PIN

Handling Edge Cases.

Proactive delay alerts and foolproof doorstep handovers.

When real-world conditions change, Saffron speaks up before the user starts worrying.

  • ✓ Transparent Activity Feed (s11.png): An event log records every milestone: order placed, kitchen prep completed, rider transit, and traffic recalculation.
  • ✓ Calm Bottom Banner (s12.png): Persistent reassurance bar sits quietly at the screen base while the user explores other app features.
  • ✓ Doorstep 4-digit PIN (s14.png): Direct handover code guarantees food is never handed to the wrong neighbor in a shared hallway.
